Do not attach photos or .wpz files to the report If you receive a puzzle that breaks these rules, use Settings → Report or the report action on that item in Received. Incoming files stay on the receiving device; we do not host them. By tapping Agree you confirm you understand these rules and will not share illegal or pornographic material.
