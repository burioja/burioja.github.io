Last updated: 2026-11-02 SWAP PUZZLER ("the App") is operated by burioja. The App is available on Android (Google Play) and iOS (App Store). Contact: swappuzzler@gmail.com 1. Data stored on your device • Game progress, settings, language, inventory, and purchase entitlements stay on your device. • Optional photos or videos you pick for puzzles stay on the device unless you explicitly share a puzzle file (.wpz). • On Android, you choose one folder with the system folder picker. The App keeps the system-granted folder URI on this device and reads only supported photos and videos in that folder on later launches; it does not request broad photo-library access. You can change the folder in Settings or revoke access in Android settings. On iOS you may grant limited or full Photos access. If access is unavailable, bundled sample photos and short videos that ship with the App are used. Those samples stay in the App's own files and are not added to your device gallery. 2. Sharing • You may export a puzzle file through the system share sheet (Android or iOS). We do not upload that file to our servers and we do not run a public feed. • A file you send is visible to the person you choose. Incoming .wpz files are saved in the Received list on that device so you can play, delete, or report them. They are not uploaded to our servers. 3. Where Google requires it, that control reopens the UMP form so you can change ad consent. In other regions Google may not show a form. • Buying No Ads (or restoring that purchase) stops ads inside the App. 4. Analytics • We do not collect usage analytics and we do not send analytics events. • We do not share analytics data with advertising or analytics partners, because we do not generate it. • We do not sell your personal photos. • If we start sending analytics later, we will update this policy first. 5. Consumable items (gold, crystals, stones, coupons, starter/booster packs) cannot be restored after they have been used. • Refunds follow Apple or Google rules. 6.
